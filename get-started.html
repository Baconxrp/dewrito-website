1 Create your account

Matrix and Fluxer use separate accounts. Start with one — you can add the other later.

Matrix

The open, federated protocol. Full control, end-to-end encryption, and works across the wider Matrix network.

Pros
  • Decentralized — no single company controls it
  • End-to-end encryption in private rooms
  • Works with many clients and bridges
Cons
  • Can feel slower in very large rooms
  • Client experience varies
  • Slightly steeper learning curve
Register on Sable →

Registration happens through Sable, our hosted web client. Your Matrix ID will be @yourname:dewrito.net.

Fluxer

A Discord-style app with channels, roles, voice/video, and screen sharing — fully self-hosted.

Pros
  • Familiar Discord-like experience
  • Lightweight and fast
  • Privacy-focused, no ads
Cons
  • Newer project (still maturing)
  • No federation yet
  • Separate account from Matrix
Join via invite →

Download apps

In the apps, use fluxer.dewrito.net as the instance.

Write down your password.
Matrix encrypts private rooms on your device. If you lose both your password and your recovery key, nobody (including us) can recover your encrypted history.

4 Secure your account

  • Set up key backup — Enable Secure Backup and save the recovery key in a password manager.
  • Verify new devices — Use the emoji verification when you sign in somewhere new.
  • Notifications (optional) — Turn them on in the mobile app if you want pings.

Good to know

  • Bridges — Discord bridge is available. Ask in the general room.
  • Reporting — Use the client report feature or email abuse@dewrito.net.
  • Status — Check the status page if something feels offline.
  • Help — Contact us anytime.
