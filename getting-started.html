Getting Started

From zero to chatting

Dewrito runs two chat services. Matrix is the open, federated network where most of our community lives. Fluxer is a self-hosted, Discord-style app. You can use one or both. This guide takes about ten minutes.

Before you begin: you need an email address from a provider on our allowed list. There is a checker on the register page. Accounts are for people 18 and over, per the terms.

Create your Matrix account Matrix

Registration happens through Sable, the web client we host. It is pre-pointed at our homeserver, so you only fill in a username, password and email.

Register on Sable →

Check your inbox for the verification email and click the link. Your full Matrix ID will be @yourname:dewrito.net. That is the address people use to find you anywhere on the Matrix network, not only on our server.

Write down your password. Matrix encrypts private rooms on your device. If you lose both your password and your recovery key (step 4), nobody, including us, can recover your encrypted history.

Pick a client

Matrix is a protocol, so any Matrix app works with your Dewrito account. When an app asks for your homeserver, enter dewrito.net. The app discovers the rest on its own.

Join the Dewrito space

A space is a folder of rooms. Our official space holds the general chat, announcements and the rooms our members run. Join it and everything else is one click away.

Join #dewrito:dewrito.net →

  • The link opens a matrix.to page that hands you to whichever client you installed. On Sable, paste #dewrito:dewrito.net into the search box instead.
  • Say hello in #dewrito_general:dewrito.net, the main chat room.
  • Open the space in your client and pick the rooms you want to see. Leaving a room never leaves the space.

Looking for communities outside our space? The directory lists rooms across the Matrix network and Fluxer servers, with member counts and descriptions.

Secure your account

Do this once, in the first client you set up. It takes a minute and protects your encrypted messages.

  • Set up key backup. In your client's security settings, enable "secure backup" and save the recovery key somewhere safe, such as a password manager.
  • Verify new devices. When you sign in somewhere new, your existing session asks you to verify it with emoji. Do it. Unverified sessions cannot read your encrypted history.
  • Turn on notifications in the mobile app settings if you want pings for mentions and direct messages.

Try Fluxer Fluxer

Fluxer is a separate account from Matrix. It feels like Discord: servers, channels, roles, voice and video, screen sharing. Nothing federates to other instances, and nothing leaves our hardware.

Good to know

  • Bridges. A Discord bridge bot runs on our homeserver. If you run a Discord server and want a room bridged, ask in the general room.
  • Reporting. Use your client's report feature, or email abuse@dewrito.net. Our moderation page explains what happens next.
  • Service problems? Check the status page before assuming it is your connection.
  • Stuck? Contact us. Registration and email-whitelist questions are the most common, and we answer them.
